How the score works, and what was excluded

Every institution listed here is reachable by phone, email or website. Anything with no contact of any kind was removed outright, along with franchises tied to a competitor and off-target verticals (salon, culinary, automotive, music).

Coverage is complete. All 12,934 private LKPs in the Kemendikdasmen registry, across every province, have been scanned and name-classified. Contact details were then pulled for every on-target candidate. Java holds the bulk of the opportunity: Jawa Barat, Jawa Timur, Jawa Tengah and DKI Jakarta account for roughly 60% of all prospects.

What the score rewards

  • Audience over curriculum. An institution whose learners are students or career-track adults is a CPNS audience whatever it teaches today.
  • Already sells civil-service prep (+35) · is a bimbel (+22) · serves a career/professional cohort (+18) · runs a computer lab (+16)
  • Reachability and digital capability: website, own email domain, phone and email
  • Light penalty for degree-granting institutions and chains with their own curriculum — a subscription add-on does not clash with their programme the way a franchise would

Bands

  • 55+ strong · 40–54 good · 28–39 worth a call · under 28 long tail
  • Hover any score to see its reasoning. Treat it as a ranking guide, not a gate.

Caveats

  • Segments are inferred from institution names and email addresses — the registry’s own vertical field is empty for every record. Treat as a first pass.
  • Scores below 40 lean on name-based inference, so expect a lower hit rate there.
  • ★ marks a recognisable operator or a direct civil-service-prep signal.
