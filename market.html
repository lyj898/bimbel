Why this map is separate from the registry. Only 152 of the 2,594 registry LKPs are bimbel, and the biggest players are largely absent — Ruangguru appears twice, Akses Learning and CPNS Academy not at all. Bimbels typically incorporate as PT/CV rather than register as LKP under Kemendikdasmen, so the registry cannot map this sector. A blank field means the operator does not publish that figure, never that it is zero.
